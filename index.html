How It Works

Five simple steps, all online, from wherever you are -- no showroom visit, no fittings, every detail customized to you.

  1. 01

    Choose full suit or jacket only, then design your jacket

    Start with a full suit ($350) or just the jacket ($250), then pick your fabric and customize every detail -- collar, lapel, pockets, buttons, and more. Every option is included in the price, no surprise upcharges.

  2. 02

    Customize your pants

    Choose the fabric and style for your pants -- match your jacket or go with a contrasting fabric. Entirely up to you.

  3. 03

    Enter your measurements

    Enter your finished measurements in centimeters -- or estimate from your usual size in seconds -- so the suit is cut to fit you, not sized off a rack.

  4. 04

    Personal info and shipping

    Tell us your name and where to ship your finished suit.

  5. 05

    Submit your order

    Review your selections and submit. Your final invoice is confirmed with you directly before production begins.
