How It Works

Six simple steps, all online. No showroom visit and no fittings needed.

  1. Step 1

    Choose what you're building

    A full suit from $350, a jacket only from $265, or pants only from $150. Prefer a classic look with fewer choices? Start from a ready-made preset suit or jacket.

  2. Step 2

    Design every detail

    Pick your fabric, which sets the price, then choose the lapel, pockets, buttons, lining and more. Add a monogram, or upload your own photo for the lining (+$80). For the pants, match your jacket fabric or go with a contrast. Every style option is included except full canvas (+$200).

  3. Step 3

    See your suit before you buy

    Get a free picture of your finished suit with all your choices, so you know exactly what you're ordering. Sign in to create one.

  4. Step 4

    Add your measurements

    Enter the sizes of a suit that fits you well, or measure your body and pick a fit and our tailors do the rest. You can also estimate from your usual size in seconds. We check every number is in a sensible range, and if you have an account your measurements are saved for next time.

  5. Step 5

    Choose delivery and pay

    Ship to your door at the cheapest carrier rate, or pick up in store for free. Add Alteration Protection for $100 to get three free alterations. Pay securely by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna, and you'll get a confirmation right away.

  6. Step 6

    Follow your suit and reorder anytime

    In My Account, watch your suit move from cutting and sewing all the way to delivery, with a tracking link once it ships. Love it? Reorder any past order in one tap.
