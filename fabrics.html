Fine wool suitings in solids, pinstripes, herringbones and plaids. Every fabric is included in the price, a full suit for $350 or a jacket for $250, with no upcharges. Pick yours when you design your suit.

Italian Super 120s Wool

Every Scott's Suits fabric is a fine Italian wool woven from Super 120s yarn. The "120s" grade measures how fine the wool fibers are, and at this level they are fine enough to give the cloth a soft hand and a smooth, rich drape, while staying strong enough for a suit you wear often.

The result is a fabric that feels light and comfortable, breathes well through the day, and recovers from creases so your suit keeps its clean lines from morning meetings to evening events. It is the kind of cloth found in fine tailoring, chosen so your made-to-measure suit looks and feels as good as it fits.
