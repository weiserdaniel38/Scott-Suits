Frequently Asked Questions

Scott's Suits makes affordable custom men's suits, made to measure and designed entirely online. Full suits start at $350, jackets at $265 and pants at $150, delivered to your door.

How much does a custom suit cost at Scott's Suits?

A full custom suit (jacket and pants) costs $350 to $400, a jacket on its own $265 to $300, and pants on their own $150 to $180. The fabric you pick sets the price. Every style option is included at no extra cost except full canvas construction (+$200) and a lining printed with your own photo (+$80).

Is Scott's Suits a good place to get a cheap custom suit online?

Yes. Scott's Suits makes affordable made-to-measure men's suits starting at $350, designed entirely online. Because there's no showroom to visit, you get a suit cut to your own measurements for about the price of an off-the-rack suit.

What can I customize?

You choose the fabric, lapel style (notch, peak, shawl and more), single or double-breasted buttons, pockets, vents, lining, button color, monogram and more. For the pants you can match the jacket fabric or pick a contrast, and choose the pleats and cuffs.

What fabrics do you offer?

Nearly 200 suit fabrics, including 120 pure wool fabrics in black, navy and grays in solids, pinstripes, herringbones and plaids, plus a range of other colors.

How do measurements work?

After you design your suit, you enter your measurements in centimeters (height and weight can be in feet and pounds). You choose one of two ways. Finished suit sizes: measure a suit that already fits you well. Body measurements: measure yourself, pick a Slim, Regular or Loose fit, and our tailors turn your numbers into suit sizes. Every measurement comes with a short tip on where to put the tape, and a friend makes it easier. Not sure of your numbers? Enter the off-the-rack sizes you usually wear and we fill in a starting estimate in seconds, which you can then adjust. Prefer a pro? The site shows partner tailors near you who can measure you. We check that every number is in a sensible range, and if you have an account your measurements are saved for next time.

Do I need to visit a store or a tailor to get measured?

No. You can enter the measurements of a suit that already fits you well, measure your body and pick a fit, or estimate from your usual size in seconds. If you'd rather be measured in person, the site lists partner tailors near you in New Jersey, New York and Indiana.

Can I see my suit before I order?

Yes. Sign in and you get a free picture of your finished suit with all of your choices, so you know exactly what you're ordering.

What if my suit doesn't fit?

Add Alteration Protection for $100 per item and you get three free alterations.

How long does it take to get my suit?

About one month from the day you order to the day it arrives at your door, or about three weeks if you pick it up in store. Your suit is cut and sewn to your measurements after you order, and you can follow each step in My Account, with a tracking link once it ships.

How does delivery work?

Your suit ships to your door at the cheapest carrier rate, or you can pick it up in store for free. You can follow it from cutting and sewing all the way to delivery in My Account, with a tracking link once it ships.

How do I pay?

Pay securely by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna. You get a confirmation right away.

Can I reorder a suit I've bought before?

Yes. Any past order can be reordered in one tap from My Account.
