Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom suit cost at Scott's Suits?

A full suit is $350 to $470, a jacket $275 to $360, pants $150 to $180. The fabric sets the price. All style options are included except full canvas (+$200) and a photo lining (+$80).

Is Scott's Suits a good place to get a cheap custom suit online?

Yes. Made to measure suits start at $350, designed online. With no showroom, you pay about what an off-the-rack suit costs.

What can I customize?

Fabric, lapel (notch, peak, shawl and more), single or double breasted, pockets, vents, lining, buttons, monogram and more. Pants can match or contrast, with your choice of pleats and cuffs.

What fabrics do you offer?

Nearly 200 suit fabrics, almost all pure wool, in black, navy and grays plus a range of other colors, in solids, pinstripes, herringbones and plaids.

Why do some fabrics cost more?

Fabric is the only thing that changes the base price. A suit is $350 in our 12 best-value fabrics and $410, $445 or $470 in the others. Each fabric shows its price, and only fabrics in stock are shown.

How do measurements work?

Enter them in centimeters after you design. Measure a suit that fits you well, or measure your body and pick Slim, Regular or Loose. Not sure? Estimate from your usual size, or find a partner tailor near you. Your account saves them for next time.

Do I need to visit a store or a tailor to get measured?

No. Measure a suit that fits, measure yourself, or estimate from your usual size. Partner tailors in New Jersey, New York and Indiana can also measure you.

Can I see my suit before I order?

Yes. Sign in for a free picture of your finished suit.

What if my suit doesn't fit?

Add Alteration Protection for $100 per item and you get three free alterations.

How long does it take to get my suit?

About one month to your door, or about three weeks for store pickup. Follow each step in My Account.

How does delivery work?

We ship at the cheapest carrier rate, or pick up free in store. You get a tracking link once it ships.

How do I pay?

Pay securely by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna. You get a confirmation right away.

Can I reorder a suit I've bought before?

Yes. Any past order can be reordered in one tap from My Account.
